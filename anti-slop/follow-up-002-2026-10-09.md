# Anti-slop follow-up 002: Posts UI and article details

Date: 2026-10-09  
Mode: After  
Approval: “Ok lest make these changes” after audit 002

## Changes

1. **Diagram theme switch:** `layouts/partials/footer.html` now saves each Mermaid source before rendering and restores it before a theme rerender. Rendering is serialized to avoid overlapping runs. The older Godot/UDP post now enables its existing Mermaid diagrams.
2. **Article-only index:** `layouts/posts/list.html` shows only the five published articles. `About Me` and `terms` have `hiddenInPostList: true`; their direct pages remain. The list uses dated editorial rows and real article links.
3. **Authored descriptions:** The five published articles have short front-matter descriptions drawn from their existing content. The index uses these instead of automatic excerpts. No statistics or project claims were added.
4. **Internal notes:** The published NodeStrike Execution article no longer renders its TODO section. The notes are preserved in `plans for future/nodeStrikeExecution-todo.md`.
5. **Article details:** `layouts/posts/single.html` separates title, deck, metadata, and edit link. `assets/css/extended/editorial.css` adds a serif reading voice, restrained spacing, and a sticky table of contents on wide screens. On narrow screens the contents control stays collapsed. Existing diagrams have captions tied to their actual content; chart captions use the same small-label treatment. Charts stay within the reading column, with their existing Enlarge action for a full view.
