# Anti-slop audit 003: Home screen

Date: 2026-10-09  
Mode: After

## Direction and scope

The home page's established direction is a centered, unframed profile with neutral colors and animated ASCII waves. `CLAUDE.md` records those preferences and rejects OS-style chrome, glass panels, mint accents, greetings, and animation controls. I treated that direction as fixed. Observed dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2. I reviewed the local production preview in Chrome at desktop width in both themes and at a 320px light-mode viewport, along with the Hugo templates and home animation code. No site implementation was changed.

## Numbered findings

1. **HIGH: The ASCII texture crosses small text and controls in light mode (R-25, contrast and resilience).** At 320px the pattern visibly runs behind the subtitle, social links, and primary buttons. Desktop light mode also places it near the Contact Me link. The small text uses `#6c6c6c`; a fully visible ASCII character at the mobile `0.28` opacity is about `#d7d7d7` over white, giving only **3.65:1** against that text, below the 4.5:1 normal-text target. The local mask reduces the effect in some places, but the screenshot shows the texture in the content area. Keep the waves at the edges and give the profile and actions a clear center zone in both themes. The button outlines are also very faint against the page, so check their 3:1 non-text contrast when making this change.

2. **LOW: Two of the three header destinations have no content (R-05, content-driven composition).** `/categories/` and `/tags/` now explain their empty state, but no published post supplies `categories` or `tags` front matter. The home header devotes two links to empty collections. Add real taxonomy data to the posts, or hide those links until the collections have entries. Keep Search, which is functional.

## Optional copy refinement

“Computer Programmer” is broad compared with the concrete work in the posts and the About page. A short line grounded in those sources would tell first-time visitors what Carlos studies or builds. This is a content choice, not a rule violation; the owner should choose the wording.

## What already works

- The portrait and name give the screen a clear focal point without adding a frame.
- The home links have real destinations, and the social icons have accessible names in the browser's accessibility tree.
- At 320px, the header, profile, buttons, and footer remain inside the viewport without visible horizontal clipping.
- `assets/js/home.js` stops animation for reduced-motion preference and when the page is hidden. The ASCII layer is marked `aria-hidden` and does not capture pointer input.
